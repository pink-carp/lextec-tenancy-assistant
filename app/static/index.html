Tenancy information assistant · comparison version (OpenAI model, no pre-written text)

For social workers in the canton of Zurich · residential tenancy · legal information, not legal advice

What this tool does: it reads your description of a tenancy problem, asks the follow-up questions that matter, finds the relevant articles and court decisions, calculates deadlines, and suggests a lane: information (1), information with open questions (2) or referral to a specialist (3). What it does not do: give legal advice, decide disputes, or replace your professional judgement. You stay responsible for what you pass on. Please do not type names or ID numbers; give the municipality instead of the street address.
