Tenancy information assistant

For social workers in the canton of Zurich · residential tenancy · legal information, not legal advice

What this tool does: it reads your description of a tenancy problem, lets you confirm the facts that decide the case, asks the follow-up questions that matter, calculates deadlines and suggests a lane: information (1), information with open questions (2) or referral to a specialist (3). Version v6 (Prüfungskern): the AI examines the case with the team's legal method (LIIT Prüfungskern 2.0.0) in nine modules: unpaid rent and payment demands, termination by the landlord, giving notice as a tenant, rent and contract changes, defects, the deposit, co-tenants, pets and house rules, and protecting the family home. The AI judges each legal element with the client's sentence; code combines the elements. For other situations the tool suggests a referral. What it does not do: give legal advice, decide disputes, or replace your professional judgement. You stay responsible for what you pass on. Please do not type names or ID numbers; give the municipality instead of the street address.
