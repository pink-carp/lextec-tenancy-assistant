1. **Legal Information — Canton of Zurich**

**Article 257d of the Swiss Code of Obligations (OR) is the governing provision.** It allows termination for unpaid rent or ancillary charges, but requires a **two-stage procedure**: first a written payment demand with a termination warning, then a separate termination if the arrears remain unpaid after the deadline.

On the stated facts, the letter **may be an Article 257d demand, not yet a termination**. Its wording, delivery date and the actual rent account must be checked before reaching a definitive conclusion.

**Unemployment, financial hardship and having children do not suspend the payment deadline.** Moreover, Article 272a(1)(a) OR excludes a judicial extension of the tenancy (*Erstreckung*) following a valid termination under Article 257d. The practical priority is therefore to secure payment or an express agreement with the landlord **before the deadline expires**, while checking the demand’s validity.

2. **Assessment checklist**

| Issue | Information required | Legal significance |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Procedural stage** | Only a payment demand, or also a termination form or court papers? | Determines which deadline and response apply. |
| **Actual arrears** | Lease, rent ledger, months claimed, ancillary charges, bank statements and previous payments. | Article 257d requires overdue rent or ancillary charges. Check disputed amounts and distinguish other charges, such as collection fees. |
| **Content of the demand** | Exact letter: amount claimed, payment period and express warning of termination. | The demand must be written, identify the arrears sufficiently and threaten termination if payment is not made. |
| **Length of payment period** | Does it allow at least 30 days? Does it state a fixed end date? | For residential premises, the statutory minimum is **30 days**. |
| **Receipt** | Actual delivery/collection date, envelope, postal tracking and collection notice. | The deadline is linked to receipt, not simply the letter’s date. Not collecting registered mail does not prevent the deadline from running. |
| **Family-home protection** | Is the tenant married or in a registered partnership, and is this the family home? Were separate notices delivered? | Article 266n OR requires separate delivery of both the payment demand with warning and the termination to the tenant and spouse/registered partner. **Children alone do not trigger this separate-delivery requirement.** |
| **Payment capacity** | Exact shortfall, available funds, unemployment benefits, pending applications and expected payment dates. | Determines whether full payment can be secured in time and whether urgent assistance is needed. |
| **Agreement with landlord** | Existing deferral, instalment agreement or waiver; exact written terms. | May affect the right to terminate. A request for instalments alone changes nothing. |

In **BGE 119 II 147**, the Federal Supreme Court held that receipt governs the start of the payment period. For an uncollected registered payment demand, receipt is generally deemed to occur on the last day of the seven-day collection period. **Do not automatically apply the same receipt calculation to a subsequent termination notice**; have its deadline checked separately.

3. **Procedure and consequences**

### **Stage A — Payment demand**

The landlord must give the residential tenant a written deadline of **at least 30 days**, coupled with a termination warning.

* **Full, timely settlement of the properly demanded arrears prevents termination on that arrears ground.** Arrange payment early enough and keep evidence of receipt.  
* **Partial payment generally does not remove the termination risk** if relevant arrears remain.  
* Negotiations, a social-assistance application or a promise to pay **do not automatically stop the deadline**.

### **Stage B — Separate termination**

If the arrears are not settled within the period, the landlord may terminate with **at least 30 days’ notice to the end of a month** under Article 257d(2) OR. For a dwelling, this is **not immediate termination**.

The termination must be written and use a **canton-approved form** under Article 266l OR. Separate delivery requirements for a family home must also be respected. Article 266o OR provides for nullity where the termination fails to comply with Articles 266l–266n.

**The tenancy does not end automatically when the payment deadline expires.** The landlord must take the second step. Conversely, paying after expiry does not automatically eliminate a termination right that has already arisen: seek express confirmation that the tenancy will continue.

The two-stage structure was confirmed in **BGE 119 II 147**. In **4A\_333/2022**, the Federal Supreme Court reiterated the statutory periods and examined whether termination had been issued after the payment period. That decision also cautions against assuming that every timing objection will succeed regardless of the tenant’s subsequent conduct.
