Legal information

**Rent arrears can lead to termination and eventually eviction, but this letter does not necessarily mean the tenancy has already ended. Do not ignore it.** Wording saying the landlord will terminate *if you do not pay* sounds like a payment warning, rather than an actual termination. Without the wording and postal dates, that remains provisional. The governing provision is **Art. 257d of the Swiss Code of Obligations (OR)**. Reduced working hours explain the difficulty, but do not suspend the obligation to pay rent.

### **Legal steps and deadlines**

| Stage | Legal requirement | Practical consequence |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **1\. Payment warning** | Under Art. 257d(1) OR, the landlord must give a written payment deadline of **at least 30 days**, with an express warning of termination. | This is the opportunity to clear the demanded arrears and prevent termination on that basis. |
| **2\. Termination** | If the arrears remain unpaid when that deadline expires, Art. 257d(2) OR permits termination with **at least 30 days’ notice to the end of a month**. | The landlord must take a separate termination step; the warning does not itself end the tenancy. |
| **3\. Formal notice** | Under Art. 266l OR, a residential landlord’s termination must use a **canton-approved form**. | Check every page and enclosure, not just the covering letter. |
| **4\. Challenge** | Under Art. 273(1) OR, a termination challenge must reach the conciliation authority **within 30 days after receipt**. | Do not wait for eviction proceedings or for negotiations to finish. |
| **5\. Eviction** | If the tenancy validly ends and the tenant stays, the landlord must obtain an eviction order and use the lawful enforcement process. | Property management cannot simply remove the tenant or change the locks itself. |

**These are separate deadlines, not a guaranteed “60 days from today”.** Zurich Courts explain the judicial eviction process; the City of Zurich identifies the Stadtammannamt as the authority carrying out enforcement.

### **Does collecting the letter late buy more time?**

**Not indefinitely.** For an Art. 257d payment warning, **BGE 119 II 147** establishes that receipt normally occurs when the registered letter is collected within the seven-day postal collection period. If it is not collected within that period, receipt is generally deemed to occur on its last day. The payment period is counted from the following day.

The same decision confirms that the landlord must wait until the payment deadline expires before issuing termination. **Do not assume that an actual termination notice follows the same postal-receipt rule as the payment warning.** Its receipt date needs separate checking.

Because the delivery and collection dates are missing, **the exact payment deadline and earliest lawful termination date cannot yet be calculated**.

### **What to do now**

1. **Keep the letter, all enclosures, envelope and postal tracking information.** Establish the deadline immediately.  
2. **Check the rent account:** which months are demanded, what was actually due, and whether any payments have been omitted. Distinguish rent and service-charge arrears from other fees.  
3. **If the demand is correct, arrange full payment before the deadline.** Pay early enough for the landlord to receive it, identify the months being paid and keep proof. A partial payment or promise to pay does not reliably prevent termination.  
4. **If full payment is impossible, contact management today.** Propose a specific payment date or instalment plan. Obtain written confirmation that they agree to defer termination while you comply; a request alone does not extend the deadline.  
5. **Consider bridge funding and advice immediately.** Help from your father is a practical option, not something you are legally required to accept. You can also contact a tenants’ advice service and Zurich Social Services to assess available assistance; do not assume financial assistance will be approved or arrive in time.  
6. **Keep current rent from falling further behind.** Clearing September and October does not resolve later unpaid rent.  
7. **If an actual termination has arrived, seek urgent advice and protect the 30-day challenge deadline.** For a flat in the City of Zurich, the relevant body is the rental conciliation authority at Bezirksgericht Zürich; Zurich Courts provide the authority directory.

**Waiting for month-end salary is safe only if it allows timely full payment, or the landlord has expressly agreed otherwise.** Payment after the deadline does not automatically remove the landlord’s right to terminate under Art. 257d OR.

### **Important limit: hardship does not secure an extension**

Under **Art. 272a(1)(a) OR**, a statutory extension of the tenancy is excluded for a valid rent-arrears termination under Art. 257d OR. Financial hardship is therefore not, by itself, a way to obtain extra time from the court.

**The priority is to preserve the tenancy now through timely payment or an express agreement—not to rely on a later hardship extension.**
