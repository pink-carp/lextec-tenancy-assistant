**Social Service Contacts as they Relate to the Escalation Indices**

The following referral database is designed to be part of the output when a relevant index of urgent human referral is detected.

Appropriate social services should be included in the output for a client when such services would be useful for their situation. I have included the contact information, as well as a referral statement to provide context as to what assistance that specific organization provides.

Legal urgency and severity of consequences can use the same service, but the message should explain the particular reason for referral.

A referral is not automatically an emergency referral. Some organisations provide specialist counselling or legal advice by appointment. The system should distinguish those from services that can respond to immediate danger.
